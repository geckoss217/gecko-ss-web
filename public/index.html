FAQ

Questions partners ask on the first call

Our compliance consultant already gave us an AI policy. Why do we need this?

Most firms have the policy. Fewer than half have the review steps, and about a third test outputs. The policy is the easy part. We make the day-to-day match it and produce the records that prove it.

Which tools do you push?

None. We work with what your custodian, BD, or IT provider already approves: Microsoft Copilot, Google Gemini, Claude, or a compliant notetaker. If your firm has no approved tool yet, we help you pick one and document why.

We're a small team. Isn't this overkill?

A firm with three to ten investment professionals can't justify an AI operating partner. That's the gap. Ninety days gives you a working process, a policy you can attach to a DDQ, and a playbook you can run again in a portfolio company.

Can you work inside our portfolio companies too?

Yes. The same 90-day structure runs inside a portco, starting in finance or operations. Most firms pilot it in one company, then decide whether to roll it out.

What do you need from us?

Partner attendance at the sessions, one internal champion, and access to your Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace admin (or your IT provider's cooperation). Everything else we bring.

Is our data safe with you?

Your data stays in your accounts. We don't take copies. Every tool we recommend is reviewed for training use, retention, and data residency, and that review goes in your vendor file.

What happens after 90 days?

You'll have a working workflow, a playbook, and a report. Many firms keep us on for a monthly check-in and the next workflow. Some run the playbook themselves. Either is a good outcome.
