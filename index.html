FAQ

Questions owners ask on the first call

Will customers know they're talking to AI?

Yes when it matters. The AI identifies itself when asked and hands off to a person for anything it can't handle. Customers care about a fast answer far more than who typed it.

Do I have to change my CRM or phone system?

Usually no. We connect to what you have: Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, Workiz, HubSpot, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, RingCentral, and most others. If your current setup is the real problem, we'll say so.

What does the $1,000 a month actually cover?

Building, running, and improving the systems. Think of it as a part-time operations and automation person who never calls in sick. Third-party software (your phone line, your CRM, AI usage fees) is billed to you directly and is typically under a few hundred dollars a month.

How fast will I see something working?

The first system is live within 30 days of kickoff. Missed-call text back and quote follow-up are usually the fastest wins and often go live in the first two weeks.

Is there a contract?

Ninety days to start, so there's time to build and prove it. Month to month after that.

Is my customer data safe?

Your data stays in your accounts. We use business-tier AI services that don't train on your data, and we document every connection so you can see exactly what talks to what.
